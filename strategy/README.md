# strategy/

Goals, the debt plan, and what "on track" means. `what_if` answers should respect what's written here.

**Goes here**
- Goals and their reasons.
- The budget: how it works and why ([budget.md](budget.md)).
- The debt payoff plan and its order.
- What counts as on track, and what would change the plan.

**Doesn't go here**
- Balances, APRs or minimums. Those live in `ref.debts` and the calc tables.
- Stories about what happened. Those go in `life/`.
