   Neither is decided. When one comes up, run `what_if` and show what it does to the debt-free date.

## Retirement

- He's 41 and wants to think about retirement now rather than later.
- His gut says military pay, the 401k and any other income will cover retirement.
- **The 401k is left to wealth management**, so it isn't tracked here. It's not off limits: weigh every option, 401k included, for the best judgment call.
- He's keeping contributions going. Lincoln matches 6%. He contributes 8%, rising 1% each year up to a set cap, so 9% next year.
- His yearly raise has usually been more than 1%, so each step-up has been a wash on take-home pay so far.
- There is a loan against the 401k, taken to help with the office build. It comes out of his pay automatically, so it isn't tracked here either.
