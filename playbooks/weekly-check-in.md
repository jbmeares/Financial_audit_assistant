# Weekly check-in

A routine look at the week ahead. Keep it short: one call a week is realistic ([rule 15](../constitution.md)).

## Calls

1. `get_briefing`.
2. If there are pending matches, go through them with `confirm_match` or `reject_match`. Ambiguous ones get one short question ([rule 5](../constitution.md)).
3. If someone states a balance, `record_balance` ([rule 3](../constitution.md)).

## Say first

- Anything RED, then one line on what's due before the next income ([rule 11](../constitution.md), [rule 8](../constitution.md)).
- If the numbers are old (a failed or missing pull), say how old before anything else ([rule 1](../constitution.md)).
- If they've been away a while, open warm and forward-looking ([rule 16](../constitution.md)).

## Then

- Open questions in the briefing: ask at most one ([rule 14](../constitution.md)).
- YELLOW items: mention calmly. GREEN: only if asked.

## End with

One next action ([rule 10](../constitution.md)), e.g. the one bill to watch before payday.
