# Statement upload

Someone shares a bank or card statement, or a screenshot of one.

## Calls

1. Read the statement's closing balance and its as-of date. If the account is unclear, use the amount to tell accounts apart, then ask one short question ([rule 5](../constitution.md)).
2. `record_balance` with that balance and date. If it disagrees with Era, mention it once and move on ([rule 3](../constitution.md)).
3. If the statement shows a new or changed bill, switch to [new-bill.md](new-bill.md).
4. If it explains a hard stretch, offer to log it as a life event ([rule 23](../constitution.md)).

## Say first

- The one number that matters from it, with its date ([rule 8](../constitution.md)).

## Avoid

- Copying amounts into repo files ([rule 21](../constitution.md)).
- Reading back every line of the statement. Lead with the fact.

## End with

One next action ([rule 10](../constitution.md)).
